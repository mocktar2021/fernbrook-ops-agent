# Fernbrook Outdoor Supply: Return Policy

*Synthetic document for a fictional company. Last updated for this project's purposes; not a real retail policy.*

## Standard Return Window

Most items purchased from Fernbrook Outdoor Supply, whether online or through a wholesale account, may be returned within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund to the original payment method, provided the item is unused, unwashed, and in its original packaging with tags attached.

## Category Exceptions

**Footwear.** Footwear may be returned within 30 days if it has only been worn indoors to test fit. Footwear with visible signs of outdoor wear cannot be returned but may qualify for a warranty claim; see the Warranty Policy document.

**Apparel (jackets, base layers, technical clothing).** Standard 30-day window applies. Apparel purchased during a seasonal clearance sale (marked "Final Sale" at checkout) is not eligible for return, only for a warranty claim if the item is defective.

**Tents, sleeping bags, and other camping gear.** These items may be returned within 30 days if the packaging has been opened only to inspect the item, not to set it up or use it outdoors. Gear that shows any sign of outdoor use is not eligible for a standard return but may qualify for a warranty claim.

**Extended-window exception.** Items purchased between November 1 and December 24 are eligible for return through January 31 of the following year, regardless of the standard 30-day window, to accommodate gift purchases.

## Wholesale Account Returns

Wholesale partner accounts operate under a separate 60-day return window for unsold inventory, subject to the terms of each partner's wholesale agreement. Wholesale returns must be initiated through the partner's account representative, not through the standard retail return process.

## What Voids a Return

A return is not eligible, under any of the windows above, if:

- The item has been used outdoors beyond fit-testing or packaging inspection.
- Tags or original packaging have been discarded.
- The item was marked "Final Sale" at the time of purchase.
- More than 30 days (or 60 for wholesale, or through January 31 for the holiday exception) have passed since delivery.

## How Refunds Are Issued

Approved returns are refunded to the original payment method. Refunds for orders under $100 are typically processed automatically upon return receipt. Refunds above $100 are reviewed by a member of the Fernbrook support team before being issued.
