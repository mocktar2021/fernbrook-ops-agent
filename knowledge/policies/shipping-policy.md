# Fernbrook Outdoor Supply: Shipping Policy

*Synthetic document for a fictional company. Last updated for this project's purposes; not a real retail policy.*

## Standard Shipping

Retail orders ship within 1-2 business days of being placed and typically arrive within 5-7 business days via standard ground shipping, depending on destination. Fernbrook ships to all 50 U.S. states; international shipping is not currently offered for retail orders.

## Expedited Shipping

Expedited shipping (2-3 business day delivery) is available at checkout for an additional fee. Expedited orders placed before 1 PM Pacific ship the same business day.

## Order Delays

An order is considered delayed if it has not shipped within 3 business days of being placed, or if it has not arrived within 3 business days of its original estimated delivery date. Delays are most commonly caused by high-demand items being temporarily out of stock or by carrier disruptions outside Fernbrook's control. Customers with a delayed order are entitled to a shipping fee refund if expedited shipping was purchased; delays do not otherwise affect standard order refund eligibility.

## Wholesale Shipping

Wholesale orders ship according to the schedule specified in each partner's wholesale agreement, typically weekly or biweekly consolidated shipments rather than per-order shipping. Wholesale partners with an urgent restocking need can request expedited fulfillment through their account representative, subject to inventory availability.

## Address Changes

An order's shipping address can be changed only before the order has shipped. Once an order shows a status of "shipped," Fernbrook cannot redirect it; the customer will need to contact the carrier directly or arrange to receive the original shipment and initiate a return if it arrives at the wrong address.

## Lost or Missing Packages

A package is considered lost, not merely delayed, if it has not arrived within 10 business days of its estimated delivery date and tracking shows no movement in that time. Lost packages are eligible for a full reshipment or refund once confirmed lost, at the customer's preference.
