# Fernbrook Outdoor Supply: Warranty Policy

*Synthetic document for a fictional company. Last updated for this project's purposes; not a real retail policy.*

## General Coverage

Fernbrook Outdoor Supply warrants that its house-brand products (items sold under the Fernbrook, Trailhead, Ridgeline, Cascade, and Summit product lines) are free from defects in materials and workmanship for **one year from the date of purchase**. This warranty covers manufacturing defects. It does not cover damage from normal wear, misuse, improper storage, or accidents.

## What's Covered

- Seam failure or stitching defects not caused by misuse.
- Zipper failure under normal use within the warranty period.
- Waterproofing failure on rated waterproof items within the first year, if the item has been cared for according to its included care instructions.
- Structural failure of tent poles, backpack frames, or similar hard components under normal use.

## What's Not Covered

- Damage from abrasion, punctures, or tears caused by outdoor use, terrain, or wildlife.
- Fading or discoloration from sun exposure over time.
- Damage from improper cleaning (for example, machine-washing a product labeled hand-wash only).
- Normal wear on high-friction areas such as boot soles or pack straps.
- Any modification made to the product after purchase.

## Third-Party Brands

Fernbrook carries a limited selection of third-party gear alongside its house brands. Third-party products are covered by the manufacturer's own warranty, not Fernbrook's. Fernbrook can assist in initiating a manufacturer warranty claim but does not issue refunds or replacements directly for third-party items.

## Filing a Warranty Claim

Warranty claims require the original order number and are reviewed on a case-by-case basis. Approved claims may result in a repair, a replacement, or store credit, at Fernbrook's discretion, depending on product availability. Warranty claims are not the same as returns; an item past the return window may still be eligible for a warranty claim if it has a genuine manufacturing defect.

## Relationship to the Return Policy

A customer within the standard return window who has a defective item may choose either a standard return (see the Return Policy document) or a warranty claim. Once the return window has closed, a warranty claim is the only path available for a defective item, and only genuine manufacturing defects are covered from that point forward, not general wear.
